# Prompt Engineering From Zero

> Learn prompt engineering by watching a beginner learn it in public. Every lesson shows a **weak prompt, a strong prompt, and why the difference matters**. Updated daily.

![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-green) ![Lessons](https://img.shields.io/badge/lessons-3-blue) ![Updated](https://img.shields.io/badge/updated-daily-orange) ![PRs welcome](https://img.shields.io/badge/PRs-welcome-brightgreen)

## Who this is for

- Complete beginners who have used ChatGPT or Claude but get hit-or-miss answers
- Students, analysts and developers who want a repeatable method, not tricks
- Anyone who learns better from **before/after examples** than from theory

## How each lesson works

1. **The technique**, in plain English
2. **Weak prompt** and what it produced
3. **Strong prompt** and what it produced
4. **Why it works**
5. **Copy-paste template**
6. **Try it yourself** exercise
